Understand the issue

What is air pollution?

Air pollution happens when harmful gases, smoke, dust, or tiny particles enter the air and make it unsafe for people, animals, and the environment.

🚗

Vehicle emissions

Cars, buses, and trucks release pollutants when they burn petrol or diesel. Traffic congestion can make local air quality worse.

🏭

Industries & power

Factories and power plants can release smoke and gases, especially when fuels are burned without effective pollution controls.

🔥

Burning & dust

Waste burning, construction dust, road dust, and crop-residue burning can add harmful particles to the air.

Solutions that matter

Cleaner air needs collective action.

Governments, businesses, schools, and residents all have a role in reducing pollution at its source.

🚌

Choose cleaner travel

Walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transport when practical. Keep vehicles maintained and avoid unnecessary idling.

🌳

Protect green spaces

Care for existing trees, support native planting, and keep parks and community spaces healthy. Trees help, but cannot replace emission cuts.

♻️

Never burn waste

Sort waste, reuse materials, compost suitable organic waste, and use local collection systems instead of open burning.

⚡

Save energy

Switch off unused devices, choose efficient appliances, and support cleaner energy where available.

😷

Reduce exposure

Check trusted local air-quality updates. On polluted days, reduce strenuous outdoor activity if you are at risk or feel unwell.

📢

Raise awareness

Share reliable information, report open waste burning through local channels, and encourage clean-air practices at school.
